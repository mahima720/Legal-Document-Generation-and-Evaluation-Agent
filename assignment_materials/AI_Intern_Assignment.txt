AI INTERN ASSIGNMENT 
Legal Document Generation & Evaluation Agent 
 
1. Overview 
Legal professionals work with highly structured documents such as Affidavits, Written Arguments, 
Plaints, Applications, Indexes and Memoranda. These documents follow recurring structural and 
formatting patterns. Generating correct text is not enough: the information must appear in the correct 
place, and the finished document must stay consistent with the required format. 
Your task is to build a small AI -powered Legal Document Generation Agent. It takes a sample legal 
document as a reference, accepts case-specific information, generates a new document following the 
reference structure, and then evaluates the generated document for accuracy, completeness and 
consistency. 
2. Problem Statement 
For this assignment you will work with an Affidavit in Reply. You are given a reference document and 
a separate set of case information. Your system should use the reference to understand the expected 
structure and produce a new Affidavit in Reply populated with the supplied information. 
The purpose is not to test legal kno wledge. All legal facts, entities and substantive content are 
supplied. The focus is on building a reliable AI workflow for extraction, mapping, generation and 
evaluation.

5. Scope — Keep It Small 
You are not expected to build a legal drafting platform. For this assignment: 
● Support one document type only: Affidavit in Reply. 
● Use the supplied reference document rather than designing a format from scratch. 
● Use the supplied case facts and reply points. Do not do independent legal research. 
● A para-wise reply (answering the petition paragraph by paragraph) is out of scope. 
● You may use any LLM, Python library, framework or document-generation approach. 
● The goal is a working proof of concept, not a production system. 
6. Expected Output 
The generated document should broadly preserve the reference format, including: 
● Forum and jurisdiction heading 
● Case / cause number 
● Cause title and party labels 
● Affidavit title naming the answering respondent 
● Deponent clause 
● Numbered reply paragraphs 
● Exhibit reference 
● Prayer

8. Evaluation Report 
Your workflow should produce a short evaluation report. The exact scoring method is up to you — any 
defensible scheme is acceptable, provided you explain it. It should contain: 
● An overall score. 
● Individual scores for the major evaluation dimensions. 
● A list of errors and issues detected. 
● The source or reference for each detected issue, where practical. 
● A short explanation of how the score was calculated.
